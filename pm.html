How it works

Two layers. One connected strategy.

Most vendors manage performance in silos, one product at a time. Our model connects both levels so individual tool improvements compound into business-wide outcomes.

Enterprise Level

Whole dealership outcomes

We step back from individual products and look at the entire operation, how marketing, sales, service, and F&I work together, and where the biggest leverage points are.

  • Cross-solution strategy across CRM, digital, inventory, fixed ops, and F&I
  • Revenue growth and operational efficiency as the success measures
  • Customer experience tracked end-to-end, not just within one touchpoint
  • Designed to solve broader business challenges, not product-level gaps
Solution Level

Tactical optimization per tool

Each product is managed individually to drive its specific impact, with clear KPIs and workflows. This is where the day-to-day work happens that feeds into the bigger picture.

  • Adoption and utilization tracked per product
  • Feature usage and workflow optimization within each solution
  • KPI improvement measured against your baseline and peer group
  • Targeted, measurable improvements tied to specific tools
⇄

The two layers are always connected. Solution-level wins feed enterprise-level outcomes. When your CRM close rate improves, it shows up in your revenue. When your service retention climbs, it reduces inventory acquisition cost. Your PM connects those dots.

What to expect

A consistent cadence, not a check-in call.

Every PM engagement follows the same five-part rhythm, repeated each cycle so nothing slips and progress compounds over time.

Step A
Goal Setting

Goals defined at both enterprise and solution level. Aligned to your dealership KPIs, not generic software targets.

Step B
Engagement

Regular touchpoints every ~45 days, or more frequently based on what your store needs. Not just performance reviews, but strategy sessions and training too.

Step C
Data Review

Your PM arrives with your data already analyzed. What's working, what's slipping, where opportunity exists, specific to your store.

Step D
Recommendations

Every engagement results in clear recommendations based on best practices and defined next steps with ownership assigned, not a list of suggestions.

Step E
Tracking

Activity, goals, and progress are documented and tracked. New objectives are created as existing ones are completed, so improvement never stalls.
