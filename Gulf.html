Free & Always Available

🏝️
The Beach — Public Access Points
Free
The anchor of every off-day. The sugar-white sand is made of pure quartz crystal and actually squeaks underfoot — the girls will love it. Early mornings before game time are perfect for a team walk, shell hunt, or just decompressing together on the water.

Best public access spots: Gulf Place, Cotton Bayou, Alabama Point East.
🐚 Shell hunting 🏰 Sandcastle contests 🌅 Pre-game walks
🚲
Gulf State Park — Free Bike Share
Free
Gulf State Park has a free bike share app — download it and ride up to 3 hours at no charge across 28 miles of trails. Ideal rest-day activity where families can go at their own pace. Keep an eye out for ospreys and alligators in their natural habitat.
📱 Download Bloom App ⏱ Up to 3 hrs free 📍 Gulf State Park🦎 Wildlife sightings

Low-Cost Group Wins

⛳
Glow-in-the-Dark Mini Golf — Jurassic Golf
~$12–15
The best team-bonding evening option on the trip. Jurassic Golf near Target does 5,000 sq ft of glow-in-the-dark mini golf — perfect after a game day when you want fun without tiring the girls out. Other courses in the area include Pirate's Island Adventure Golf and Adventure Island for variety.
🌙 Evening activity 👾 Post-game option 📍 Gulf Shores
🎡
The Wharf — Free Events + Ferris Wheel
Free–$10
Your go-to "team dinner + hang" hub. The Wharf runs free outdoor concerts, family movie nights, and live music all summer with no cover charge. Add a ride on the Wharf Ferris Wheel for sweeping views of the marina. Easy for a big group — everyone can do their own thing in the same space.
🎵 Live music nightly 🎆 Wharf Events Schedule 📍 The Wharf
🌊
The Hangout — Gulf Shores
Casual $$
A Gulf Shores institution right on the main public beach. Foam machine, table dancing, vintage décor, and a menu of seasonal seafood. Loud, fun, and very much 14U energy. A perfect team dinner destination on a game night when the girls need to celebrate (or shake off a tough loss).
🫧 Foam machine 🎶 Live entertainment 📍 101 E Beach Blvd

Worth the Splurge

💦
Waterville USA — Water & Amusement Park
~$35–45
About 15 minutes from Orange Beach, Waterville combines a full waterpark and amusement park: waterslides, wave pools, and lazy river. Save this for a mid-tournament rest day when the girls need to blow off steam. It's the perfect pressure release valve without anything competitive attached.
📍 906 Gulf Shores Pkwy 🔗 Waterville USA Site🌊 Wave pool + slides Gulf Shores
🦁
Alabama Gulf Coast Zoo
~$20–25
Best for families on an off-day. The real draw is the animal encounter add-ons — feeding lemurs, getting close to a sloth — at about $24.95 each. Easy morning activity that wraps up well before tournament games, and the perfect option for younger siblings and parents who want something different.
🦥 Sloth encounter 🐾 Lemur feeding 📍 1204 Gulf Shores Pkwy

Restaurants for Large Groups

🍋
LuLu's
Group-Friendly
The best all-ages team dinner spot on the strip. Part restaurant, part kid's paradise — climbing structure, giant sand area, face painting, and an arcade. Order at the counter and they bring food to your table, which makes coordinating large orders way less chaotic.
🔗 LuLu's Site🎮 Arcade 🏖️ Sand play area 📍 200 E 25th Ave
🌮
Cactus Cantina
Group-Friendly
Laid-back Mexican chain with three Alabama Beaches locations — one in Gulf Shores, two in Orange Beach. Classic fajitas, nachos, tacos, burritos. Great for a post-game group meal where everyone can find something fast at a reasonable price. No one leaves hungry.
📍 Nearest: 25311 Perdido Beach Blvd 🌯 Full Mexican menu Fast service
🍔
Sunliner Diner
Group-Friendly
Retro diner serving burgers, milkshakes, and all-day breakfast for lunch and dinner. Noted for being large with plenty of seating — a key factor when you're moving 12+ players and families. Affordable, reliable, and no-stress for coaches coordinating a group meal.
🥤 Milkshakes 🍳 All-day breakfast 📍 3800 Gulf Shores Pkwy
🦞
The Gulf — Waterfront Dining
Mid-Range
Built from shipping containers and recycled materials, The Gulf overlooks Perdido Pass with outdoor seating perfect for a big group. The menu updates daily with the freshest available catch. Great pick for the team celebration dinner — the setting makes it feel like an occasion.
🌅 Waterfront views 🔗 The Gulf Site📍 27500 Perdido Beach Blvd Orange Beach

Shopping & Convenience Stores

🛒
Walmart Neighborhood Market — Orange Beach
Closest
Right in Orange Beach on Perdido Beach Blvd — your most convenient daily stop for snacks, sunscreen, sports drinks, and anything you forgot to pack. Grocery, pharmacy, and pickup orders available.
📍 25241 Perdido Beach Blvd 📞 (251) 974-1810 Opens 7am daily
🛒
Walmart Supercenter — Gulf Shores
Full Store
The full Supercenter about 5–6 miles away in Gulf Shores. Pharmacy, auto care, vision center, and full grocery. Better for bigger team supply runs — coolers, first aid, extra gear, or bulk snacks for the tournament.
📍 170 E Fort Morgan Rd 📞 (251) 968-5871 Open 6am–11pm
🎯
Target — Gulf Shores
Gulf Shores Only
Note: Target is in Gulf Shores, not Orange Beach. Worth the short drive for clothing, team gear, toiletries, or anything Target does better. Grocery Drive Up available if you order ahead.
📍 Gulf Shores Pkwy 🚗 Target Gulf Shores ~10 min from OB
🛍️
Publix & Rouses — Local Grocery
Quality Picks
Both Publix and Rouses are in Orange Beach and offer grocery pickup. Publix is the better pick for fresh deli, premade meals, and higher-quality produce. Rouses is a Gulf Coast regional chain with excellent seafood and local products. Great for stocking a rental kitchen and saving big on team meals.
🥗 Prepared meals 🦐 Fresh Gulf seafood Pickup available

Outlet Shopping

👟
Tanger Outlets Foley
~15 min away
Yes — there's a Tanger right here. Located in Foley on Hwy 59 (about 15 min from Orange Beach), it's a full open-air outlet mall with 120+ stores including Nike, Under Armour, Adidas, Coach, Polo Ralph Lauren, Michael Kors, Old Navy, and TJ Maxx. Perfect for a shopping day off the beach, and the back-to-school timing in mid-July means extra savings on athletic gear. Plan 4–5 hours if you really shop.
📍 2601 S McKenzie St, Foley 🕙 Mon–Thu 10am–8pm 🕙 Fri–Sat 10am–9pm ☀️ Sun 10am–7pm 🔗 Tanger Foley Site⛳ Mini golf on-site 🍴 Food court

Medical & Urgent Care

🚑
AFC Urgent Care — Orange Beach (Closest to your stay)
Walk-In
Right on Perdido Beach Blvd in Orange Beach — the closest and most convenient urgent care to where you're staying. Walk-ins welcome, no referral needed. On-site digital X-rays, lab work, and a licensed provider on-site 7 days a week. Treats patients 6 months and up. Great for sprains, jammed fingers, minor cuts, sunburn complications, or anything that needs a quick professional look without the cost of an ER visit.

Self-pay options available for those without insurance.
📍 25775 Perdido Beach Blvd 📞 (251) 974-3004 🔗 Book / Walk-In Info⏰ Mon–Fri 8am–7pm ⏰ Sat–Sun 8am–6pm 🩻 X-ray on-site 🧪 Lab on-site
🏥
South Baldwin Medical Center — Gulf Shores Urgent Care
Backup Option
Hospital-affiliated urgent care in Gulf Shores — a stronger option for anything more serious than a minor injury. If a situation escalates beyond what AFC can handle, South Baldwin Regional Medical Center in Foley is the nearest full hospital.
📍 Gulf Shores Urgent Care 🏥 Hospital-affiliated More serious cases

Local Batting Cages

🥎
Homerun Legends (Legends Athletics) — Summerdale
Best for Teams
The top pick for a serious pre-tournament tune-up. This is a dedicated indoor training facility built specifically for travel teams visiting the area — baseball, fast-pitch softball, and slow-pitch all covered. Air-conditioned, turf floor, pitching machines included with rental. Cage rentals come with a bucket of balls, screen, and machine. They also have an open indoor turf area ideal for fielding drills. Credit packs available at a discount if multiple sessions are planned.

Coach note: Call ahead to book a team block — "both sides" team rentals are available and the facility is clearly set up to handle tournament groups.
📍 18301 Couch Plant Rd, Summerdale 📞 (251) 609-3825 ⏰ Mon–Thu 11am–9pm ⏰ Fri 11am–6pm · Sat 12–6pm 🌬️ Air-conditioned 🔗 Reserve a Cage⚾ Fast-pitch softball cage ~20 min from OB
🎳
The Factory — Gulf Shores
Fun + Cages
Claims to house the only indoor batting cages in Gulf Shores and the surrounding area. Three cages: slow-pitch baseball, fast-pitch baseball, and fast-pitch softball. The bonus here is that it's a full entertainment center — trampolines, climbing walls, arcade, dodgeball — so it doubles as a team activity day. Perfect for a late-tournament reward when you want girls to get some swings in but also just cut loose.

Heads up: Multiple reviews note the house bats can be warped. Bring your own bat.
📍 18948 Oak Rd W, Gulf Shores 📞 (251) 202-0900 ⏰ Wed 1–8pm · Thu 3–8pm ⏰ Fri 3–9pm · Sat 10am–9pm · Sun 12–7pm 🎯 3 cage options 🏋️ Full entertainment center ⚠️ Bring your own bat
🏟️
Gulf Shores Sportsplex — Tournament Facility Cages
May Be Included
The Gulf Shores Sportsplex (likely your tournament venue) has batting cages with indoor/outdoor carpet on-site. Depending on your tournament organization, these may be available during warm-up windows or between games. Check with your tournament director on cage access — it's worth asking before paying for outside rental.

Note: Steel cleats are prohibited in the cages per facility rules.

Team Budget Tips

💡 Smart Money Moves for the Trip

Cook in the rental Hit Rouses or the Gulf Shores seafood markets for fresh catch and cook family-style — a fraction of restaurant cost for a team of 12+.
Order groceries ahead Publix, Rouses, and Walmart all offer pickup. Schedule pickup for 1–2 hours after check-in so it's ready when you arrive.
Free nights at The Wharf Check the Wharf event schedule before you arrive — free concerts and movie nights run all summer and cost nothing.
Tanger Club membership Free to sign up and gives exclusive discounts across all 120 stores. Worth it if even a few families shop at Nike or Under Armour.
