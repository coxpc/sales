How it works
What happens inside the council
Step 01

You submit a challenge or idea

Got a workflow that feels broken? A question about how AI could help your team? A use case you've been curious about? Submit it through our form, no polish required.

Step 02

The council works it with Claude

Council members bring submissions into our bi-weekly sessions. We explore, brainstorm, and build solutions together using Claude, then document what we learn.

Step 03

Insights come back to the team

What we discover doesn't stay in the room. Learnings, tools, and ideas get shared back across Retail Solutions, including the vAuto team, so everyone benefits, whether or not you're in the council.
