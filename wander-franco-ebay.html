About This Listing

Welcome, fellow optimist. You are looking at a lovingly curated collection of 14 Wander Franco baseball cards, acquired between 2021 and 2023 during a period historians will call "The Before Times."

Wander Franco was, by all accounts, the most exciting shortstop prospect in a generation. A $182 million contract. Hall of Fame trajectory. The kind of player you build a collection around. And then — much like a perfect game ruined by a walk in the 8th — things went sideways. Spectacularly. Historically sideways.

The cardboard, however? Flawless. These cards have done absolutely nothing wrong. They showed up, they looked great, they did their jobs. Unlike their subject, they stayed in their sleeves.
