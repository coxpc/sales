What This Is

Data That Arrives
Before the Customer Does

Customer Indicators and Lifestyle Insights in VinSolutions are enrichment layers — external data signals layered onto a customer record to help dealers understand who a buyer is, what they care about, and when they're likely to act — before a single conversation happens.

The Analogy

Think of the CRM record like a medical chart. Without enrichment, it only shows visit history. Customer Indicators and Lifestyle Insights add the equivalent of bloodwork and family history — context the doctor needs to make the right call, even on a first visit.

The Two Engines

Adobe + Adstra: Different Jobs, One Goal

Ae
Adobe Experience Cloud
The Behavior Layer — what did they do?
  • Tracks digital behavior across OEM sites, Autotrader, KBB, Dealer.com, and third-party publisher networks
  • Assigns each shopper an Experience Cloud Identifier (ECID) that persists across sessions and devices
  • Captures vehicle configurations, VDP views, trade-in tool usage, finance calculator interactions
  • Builds a real-time behavioral profile — what they looked at, how long, and what they didn't do next
  • Powers personalization: the dealer website can show inventory aligned to what a returning visitor already browsed
  • Operates within a clean room environment — data is activated without raw data being exposed
Ad
Adstra Conexa
The Identity Layer — who are they, really?
  • Resolves fragmented signals (device, email, cookie, household) into a single unified identity
  • Appends offline data: demographic, financial, lifestyle, and life-event attributes to the profile
  • Cox Automotive uses Adstra's Conexa platform specifically for consumer data strategy — confirmed partnership
  • Identifies anonymous web visitors as real, addressable consumers before they submit a lead
  • Links the same person across Autotrader, KBB, and VinSolutions — even if they never logged in
  • Delivers audience segments and Link Quality Scores so Cox knows how confident each match is

PC Coaching Note

Lifestyle Insights don't tell dealers what to sell — they tell dealers what conversation to start. The goal is to walk into a customer interaction already knowing what matters to them, so the salesperson leads with relevance instead of guessing. That's the shift from reactive to proactive retail.

📂 Where to Find It
Customer Indicators appear on the customer record in VinSolutions — typically in the Intelligence or Insights panel on the contact detail view. Lifestyle data appears in the enrichment section below core contact fields. Not all records will have all indicators — data availability depends on identity match quality from Adstra.
🎯 Who to Prioritize
Sort your customer base by In-Market Score descending. Layer in Lease/Loan End to find customers with both urgency and a natural exit point. Add Equity Position to qualify which conversations are clean pulls versus potential challenges. This stack-ranking is your proactive outreach list.
💬 What to Say
Don't reference the data directly — "I see your lease ends in 90 days" sounds surveillance-like. Instead, use it to frame the conversation: "We're reaching out to customers whose vehicles are around that 3-year mark — a lot of them are finding this a great time to explore their options." The insight drives the timing; the conversation stays human.
⚠️ Common Misuses
Don't treat Credit Tier Estimate as a hard pull — it's a model, not a bureau score. Don't skip the conversation because the In-Market Score is low — scores are probabilities, not verdicts. Don't ignore Lifestyle data just because it's not a "trigger" — it shapes the right product and pitch before the customer even arrives.

Scripts and setups for explaining Customer Indicators and Lifestyle Insights to dealer principals and sales managers.

🧩 The Setup — What Problem Does This Solve?

Use this framing before introducing the feature.
"Most dealerships are reactive. A customer walks in or submits a lead, and then you react. What Customer Indicators do is flip that model. Instead of waiting for the customer to raise their hand, you already know who's likely in-market, what they care about, and when their situation is about to change. That's the difference between a team that calls 200 random customers a week and a team that calls the 30 right ones."

🔌 Explaining the Data Sources

When a dealer asks "where does this data come from?"
"Two places. One is behavioral — what your customers are actually doing online. Are they browsing Autotrader? Configuring on the OEM site? Visiting competitor VDPs? That's Adobe tracking anonymous intent signals and tying them back to known customers. The second layer is identity data — demographic, financial, and lifestyle attributes from a data company called Adstra. Cox actually uses their platform specifically for this enrichment. So when you open a customer record and see a credit tier estimate or a life event flag, that's real third-party data that's been matched to that customer."

💡 The Lifestyle Insights Pitch

When showing the Lifestyle section of the record for the first time.
"This is what I want you to think about — before your salesperson picks up the phone, they should know something about who they're calling. If I told you this customer has three kids, lives in a rural zip, and shows an outdoor lifestyle flag, you're pitching a different vehicle than if I told you they're a business owner with a tech affinity. Lifestyle Insights let your team walk into every conversation with context. That's not magic — that's data doing its job."
💡 Tip: Pull up an actual customer record during this conversation. Live data always lands harder than slides.

🛡️ Handling the "I Don't Trust the Data" Objection

Common pushback from skeptical GMs or sales managers.
"That's fair — and I'll be direct with you. The data isn't perfect. Credit tier is modeled, not a bureau pull. In-market scores are probabilities. What they're not is noise — they're signals that help your team prioritize. You don't have to trust every data point to benefit from it. If the In-Market Score helps your BDC call the right 40 customers instead of 400 random ones, it's already earning its keep. Use it as a starting point, not a verdict."
